# lxj_fiction (archived)

The original LXJ Fiction application and EPUB sources are retained here for
reference. They are not compiled or registered. Reader owns TXT and EPUB
reading through `/sdcard/books`; its EPUB parser is under `apps/reader/epub/`.

The legacy input directory was `/sdcard/fiction`. Move books to
`/sdcard/books` to make them visible in Reader. EPUB position and cache files
remain under `/sdcard/bookmarks/epub_progress/` and
`/sdcard/bookmarks/epub_cache/` respectively.
